# Code of Conduct

This project is meant to be fun, and that only works if everyone feels welcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Welcome newcomers. Many people here are making their first contribution.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep jokes aimed at machines and software, never at people.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks
- Discrimination of any kind
- Publishing someone's private information

## Enforcement

Report problems by contacting the project maintainer. Maintainers may remove content or block people who break these rules.
